# Gendustry: CE Unofficial Port

</p>

An unofficial port of **Gendustry: Community Edition** for **Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge**.

Gendustry adds machines for working with Forestry genetics. Breed and modify bees, trees, and butterflies, automate apiaries, and build genetic templates to replicate or alter organisms.

## Features

- **Industrial Apiary** — Automate bee breeding with a mechanical hive and a range of upgrades.
- **Mutatron** — Trigger organism mutations, including an advanced version that lets you choose the result.
- **Sampler and Imprinter** — Extract alleles into Gene Samples, assemble Genetic Templates, and apply them to organisms.
- **Replicator** — Create organisms from Genetic Templates using Liquid DNA and Protein.
- **Genetic Transposer** — Copy Gene Samples and Genetic Templates.
- **DNA Extractor, Protein Liquefier, and Mutagen Producer** — Process organisms and materials into fluids used by Gendustry machines.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge
- [Forestry: Community Edition](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/forestry-community-edition)
- [Patchouli](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/patchouli)

JEI is supported for recipe viewing.
